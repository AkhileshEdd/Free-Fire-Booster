# Store listing draft

Name: Ember: Game Companion

Short description:
Prepare, check your connection and keep a personal gaming session journal.

Full description:

Build your pre-match routine with Ember, an independent companion for Free Fire and Free Fire MAX.

CHECK BEFORE YOU PLAY
See your phone's battery level, battery temperature, available memory, free storage and connection status. Spot common issues such as Battery Saver, low charge or a warm battery before a long session.

KEEP YOUR SETUP HANDY
Choose Balanced, Competitive or Endurance guidance. Save your own graphics, sensitivity and HUD notes for each supported game and profile. Apply your choices manually in the game.

UNDERSTAND YOUR CONNECTION
Run an optional 12-sample connection check against a public Cloudflare or Google endpoint. View the median connection time, sample variation and failed attempts. These tests measure that public endpoint, not the game server, and do not improve or reroute your traffic.

REMEMBER WHAT WORKED FOR YOU
Launch a supported game, then return to end your session. Record how it felt and compare setup notes with start/end battery readings. Export your journal as CSV.

ONE PURCHASE. ALL FEATURES.
No ads, subscription, account or tracking SDKs. Profiles and session records stay on your phone. Optional network tests contact your selected endpoint, which can see your IP address.

IMPORTANT BEFORE BUYING
Ember is a preparation and diagnostics toolkit. It does not unlock FPS, change game files, clean other apps, cool your phone, or guarantee lower latency or higher performance. Its elapsed session timer includes time spent outside the game until you end it. Display refresh rate is not in-game FPS; battery temperature is not CPU temperature.

Free Fire and Free Fire MAX are owned by their respective owners. Ember is not affiliated with or endorsed by Garena. Game availability depends on your region and device.
